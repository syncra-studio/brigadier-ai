# A10 battery: V2's thread and lookup batteries and a late-session quality probe in one real long session
id: a10
timeout: 18000

BUILD-PLAN Phase X, research A10: the thread's rebirth at 200k and 300k against today's 400k on a 1M window
(`a10.py`). One session per threshold, the same steps for every arm, with Brigadier's real briefing (no cap).

- `r1`–`r6` plant V2's items and `f1`–`f4` are its first fillers, as in `lookup.md`.
- `bNN` ballast: two generated files of about 20k tokens each (`a10.py template`), read in one step, so the context
  grows about 40k a step, as tool output grows a real session's. No arm passes its threshold by more than one step.
  Each reply describes the two files in about 300 words (no command, no counting), so an exchange is about the size of
  a real one (some 2.4 KB) and the briefing's verbatim tail (a 20k-token target) holds about the last 20 of them, as
  in a long session; with one-line replies it would hold the whole battery and nothing planted would leave it.
- `k1`–`k4`, the early quality probe, at about 70–90k in every arm (no arm reborn yet): V3's q1, q2, e1 and e3,
  verbatim.
- `l1`–`l4`, the late quality probe, the same step in every arm, after six more ballast steps (about 330–350k in the
  400k arm; the 300k and 200k arms have been reborn by then): V3's q3, q4, e2 and e4, verbatim. The halves are matched
  (two questions and two edits each), so a session can't copy an earlier answer, and ordered so no edit moves a later
  question's line.
- Ballast (`b08`–`b22`) then goes on until the 400k arm has been reborn twice, with two steps to spare. `f5`–`f10`
  follow, then V2's two edits: `e1` applies the JSDoc rule and its exception after the seams, once their source
  messages have left the verbatim tail (the scorer requires both: `acted_after_seams`), and `e2` avoids the ruled-out
  approach.
- `p1` is the thread battery's probe (memory only). `p2` is the lookup battery's probe, sent from a copy of the state
  before `p1` (`restore_before`: the data dir and the thread's Claude transcripts are put back), so `p1`'s answers are
  not in what it can search. Each is scored on its own. Making the copy restarts the daemon, which drops a rebirth
  prepared after the last step and not yet swapped: both probes then run in the same session.

Each request is sent verbatim, followed by the harness's safety sentence (`tools/ab/task.py`); a ballast step's
`{files}` names its two files.
