## How the probes run (same for every arm's result)

`tools/ab/probe-app.sh <arm-dir> <sha>` builds and starts that SHA's real desktop app in a detached
worktree of the arm's clone: `pnpm tauri dev --config '{"identifier":"ai.brigadier.ab-<arm>"}'`
with the daemon built from the same SHA and `BRIGADIER_DATA_DIR=<arm-dir>/probe-data`. It never
uses `ai.brigadier.app`, the installed app or its data, and `probe-app.sh --clean` removes the
identity's WebKit data, caches and defaults afterwards. The evaluator then drives the window with
computer use (accessibility tree, screenshots), at a 1280×834 window, and saves under
`<arm-dir>/probes/`: one screenshot and one accessibility-tree dump per numbered step, plus a
`probes.md` with each probe's verdict (pass / fail / can't tell, and why).

- P1.1 Choice. Hover or focus the sidebar toggle in the titlebar. A chevron is shown with it
  (or as part of it). Activating the chevron offers exactly the two closed modes, strip and fully
  hidden (whatever their labels), with the current one marked. Evidence: screenshot of the open
  choice, and its menu items in the accessibility tree.
- P1.2 Strip mode. Choose the strip mode, then close the sidebar with the toggle. The icon strip
  is shown at the window's left edge (its buttons are in the accessibility tree and on screen).
- P1.3 Fully hidden. Choose fully hidden, then close the sidebar with the toggle. No part of the
  sidebar is shown: no strip and none of its buttons in the accessibility tree; the main view
  starts at the window's left edge; the titlebar toggle is still reachable and reopens the sidebar.
- P1.4 Persisted. With fully hidden chosen and the sidebar closed, quit the app (Cmd-Q) and start
  it again with the same identity and data dir. The sidebar comes back closed in the fully hidden
  mode (or, if the arm reopens the sidebar on start, the chevron still shows fully hidden as the
  chosen mode, and closing it hides it fully).
- Pass for T1.1–T1.3: P1.1–P1.4 all pass.
