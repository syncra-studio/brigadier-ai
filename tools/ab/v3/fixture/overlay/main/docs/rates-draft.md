# Sales tax rates (draft for the accountant)

The rates tally applies to each invoice line, by region and tax category.

| Region | Standard | Reduced | Exempt |
|---|---|---|---|
| AB | 5% | 5% | 0% |
| BC | 12% | 7% | 0% |
| ON | 13% | 5% | 0% |
| QC | 14% | 5% | 0% |

Tax is computed per invoice line and rounded to cents; the invoice's tax is the sum of its lines.
